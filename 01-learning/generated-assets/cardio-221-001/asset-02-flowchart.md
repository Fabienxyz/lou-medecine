# Asset 02 — Flowchart

## Formation of an atherosclerotic plaque

```
Healthy artery
      │
      ▼
Excess LDL enters the intima
      │
      │ Why?
      │ High circulating LDL + endothelial permeability
      ▼
LDL becomes oxidized
      │
      │ Why?
      │ Free radicals modify trapped LDL
      ▼
Oxidized LDL is recognized as abnormal
      │
      ▼
Endothelium expresses adhesion molecules
      │
      ▼
Monocytes adhere to the vessel wall
      │
      ▼
Monocytes migrate into the intima
      │
      ▼
Monocytes become macrophages
      │
      ▼
Macrophages ingest oxidized LDL
      │
      ▼
Macrophages become foam cells
      │
      ▼
Inflammation amplifies itself
      │
      ▼
Smooth muscle cells migrate
      │
      ▼
Collagen + extracellular matrix are produced
      │
      ▼
Fibrous cap forms
      │
      ▼
Stable atherosclerotic plaque
```

---

## One sentence to remember

**Each step naturally causes the next one.**

Never memorize the list.

Understand the chain.
