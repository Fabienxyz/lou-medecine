# 2. Pedagogical Goal

This paragraph explains **how a healthy arterial wall progressively develops an atherosclerotic plaque**.

The objective is **not** to memorize isolated biological events.

The objective is to understand the complete biological mechanism linking LDL accumulation, inflammation, vascular remodeling and plaque formation into one coherent story.

---

# 3. Expected Learning Outcome

After studying this paragraph, Lou should be able to:

- Explain the sequence of plaque formation from beginning to end.
- Explain why each step naturally leads to the next.
- Describe the mechanism without relying on the original bullet list.
- Predict the consequence of interrupting one major step of the process.
- Reuse this mechanism later to understand plaque progression, plaque rupture and therapeutic targets.

# 5. Why is this paragraph difficult?

## For a typical student

- Many unfamiliar biological actors.
- Invisible microscopic processes.
- Dense medical vocabulary.
- Long causal chain.
- Several mechanisms occur simultaneously.
- The chronology is hidden by bullet points.
- No intuitive mental model is provided.

---

# 6. Why is this paragraph difficult for Lou?

Based on observed learning behaviour.

- She first wants to understand **why** each event occurs.
- She naturally learns through chronological sequences.
- She dislikes isolated lists to memorize.
- She builds understanding through causal reasoning.
- She benefits from visual representations.
- She often needs a small amount of background knowledge before the explanation becomes obvious.
- She wants one coherent mechanism rather than nine independent facts.

# 8. Teaching Strategy

## Primary strategy

Teach the paragraph as a chronological biological story.

## Secondary strategy

Pause after every major event and explain:

"Why did this happen?"

before continuing.

## Why this strategy?

The official paragraph already contains the correct scientific content, but its chronological structure is implicit rather than explicit.

The transformation should reveal this hidden structure.
