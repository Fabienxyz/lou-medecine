# Purpose

This project exists for one purpose:

> Help Lou prepare for the EDN by automatically transforming each official college chapter into learning materials that match the way she understands and studies.

The objective is to eliminate the manual work required to restructure the college so that Lou can spend her time understanding, memorizing and mastering medicine.

---

# Guiding Principles

## 1. The College is the Only Medical Source

The official college is the only source of medical knowledge.

The platform never replaces the college.

It augments it.

---

## 2. Knowledge Fidelity

Medical knowledge must remain faithful to the college.

The platform must never:

- invent medical facts;
- enrich the content with external medical knowledge;
- extrapolate beyond the college;
- omit medically relevant information;
- alter the scientific meaning.

The platform transforms representation, never knowledge.

---

## 3. Complete Traceability

Every medical statement, diagram or learning asset must always be traceable back to the exact location in the college from which it originates.

Trust depends on traceability.

---

## 4. Representation is Flexible

The representation of knowledge may change completely if it improves learning.

Examples include:

- mind maps
- flowcharts
- decision trees
- comparison tables
- timelines
- visual hierarchies
- consistent colour coding

Changing representation is encouraged.

Changing medical knowledge is forbidden.

---

## 5. Pedagogical Aids are Explicit

The platform may generate teaching aids such as:

- explanations;
- analogies;
- comparisons;
- guided reasoning;
- questions.

These are educational tools.

They are never presented as official medical knowledge and must remain clearly distinguishable from the college.

---

## 6. Trust Comes Before Convenience

Whenever there is a conflict between:

- richer AI-generated content;

and

- absolute fidelity to the college,

the college always prevails.

Trust is the foundation of the project.

---

# Learning Philosophy

Learning follows three successive phases.

## Phase 1 — Build the Mental Map

Understand how the chapter is organised before studying the details.

Typical learning assets:

- mind maps;
- chapter roadmaps;
- concept hierarchies.

---

## Phase 2 — Build Understanding

Understand the logic behind every paragraph.

The objective is to answer the questions that naturally arise while reading:

- Why?
- How?
- What causes this?
- What happens next?
- Why does this treatment work?

This phase currently represents the largest time investment for Lou and therefore the highest opportunity for automation.

---

## Phase 3 — Build EDN Mastery

Once understanding has been achieved, the platform helps Lou master the level of detail required for the EDN.

Typical learning assets include:

- flashcards;
- QCM;
- clinical cases;
- spaced repetition;
- EDN-specific details.

---

# Design Philosophy

The platform does not attempt to decide the single best representation.

Instead, it generates a small number of high-quality learning assets.

Lou remains in control and selects the representations that best fit her way of learning.

The platform progressively adapts to her preferences.

---

# Success

The project succeeds if it allows Lou to spend significantly less time:

- rewriting the college;
- reorganising information;
- drawing diagrams;
- creating learning material;

and significantly more time:

- understanding;
- memorising;
- revising;
- preparing for the EDN.

---

# Out of Scope (V1)

The project is not intended to:

- replace the official college;
- become a general AI tutor;
- become a commercial SaaS platform;
- support multiple users.

Its only objective is to help Lou study more effectively.
