# Asset 04 — Storyboard

Imagine watching a short animation.

## Scene 1

A healthy artery.

Blood flows normally.

The wall is clean.

---

## Scene 2

More and more LDL particles become trapped inside the wall.

Nothing is visible yet.

---

## Scene 3

The trapped LDL becomes oxidized.

A danger signal appears.

---

## Scene 4

Immune cells arrive.

The artery is now inflamed.

---

## Scene 5

Macrophages eat oxidized LDL.

They become larger and larger.

Eventually they become foam cells.

---

## Scene 6

The body tries to repair the damage.

Smooth muscle cells arrive.

Collagen is deposited.

---

## Scene 7

A fibrous cap covers the lesion.

The plaque is now established.

The artery has changed permanently.

---

## Final image

A plaque is **the result of chronic inflammation followed by imperfect repair.**
