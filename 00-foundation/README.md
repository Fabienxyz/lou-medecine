# Foundation

The Foundation layer contains the project's immutable documents.

These documents answer questions that should remain stable throughout the life of the project.

They describe:

- why the project exists;
- the principles that guide every decision;
- the vocabulary shared by the project;
- the research philosophy.

Foundation documents should evolve slowly.

Unlike product, research or architecture documents, they should not change every time a new idea emerges.

---

# Purpose

Before making any product, research or implementation decision, read this folder.

The documents in this layer define the project's direction and decision-making principles.

If another document contradicts a Foundation document, the Foundation document takes precedence.

---

# Documents

## vision.md

Defines:

- the mission;
- the problem being solved;
- the learning journey;
- the definition of success;
- the project's scope.

---

## principles.md

Defines the immutable principles of the project, including:

- the role of the official college;
- knowledge fidelity;
- traceability;
- the separation between medical knowledge and pedagogical aids;
- the learning philosophy.

---

## glossary.md *(future)*

Defines the project's shared vocabulary.

Examples include:

- college
- chapter
- learning asset
- pedagogical aid
- knowledge fidelity
- traceability

---

# Reading Order

For anyone joining the project:

1. vision.md
2. principles.md
3. glossary.md (when available)

Only then continue with the research and product documentation.
