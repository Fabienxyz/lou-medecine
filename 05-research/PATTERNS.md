# Purpose

This document contains validated learning patterns observed across multiple conversations with Lou.

Unlike `OBSERVATIONS.md`, only recurring behaviors should appear here.

Every pattern must be supported by evidence from at least two independent conversations.

Patterns are the bridge between user research and product design.

---

# P001 — Learning starts from questions, not from the chapter

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

Lou naturally starts her learning process from QCMs, annales or concrete questions rather than by reading an entire chapter linearly.

She uses questions to identify what is important before revisiting the underlying concepts.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

The companion should support question-driven learning instead of assuming a linear chapter workflow.

---

# P002 — Lou searches for transferable reasoning rules

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

Lou rarely wants the explanation for one isolated question.

She tries to discover the general reasoning rule that will allow her to answer many future variants.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Every explanation should end with a reusable reasoning rule whenever possible.

---

# P003 — Understanding always comes before memorization

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

Lou refuses to memorize information until she understands the underlying logic.

She naturally postpones memorization until she feels that the concept makes sense.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

The learning workflow must clearly separate understanding from memorization.

---

# P004 — Mechanisms are preferred over isolated facts

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

Lou repeatedly asks for mechanisms, causal chains and explanations of why something happens.

She is uncomfortable memorizing isolated facts without understanding their origin.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Mechanistic explanations should be generated before memorization assets.

---

# P005 — Lou learns by comparing similar concepts

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

When concepts are close, Lou naturally asks what differentiates them.

She learns efficiently through explicit comparisons.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Comparison tables should be considered a first-class learning asset.

---

# P006 — Contradictions must be resolved before learning continues

## Confidence

Very High

## Description

When new information appears to contradict Lou's existing understanding, she pauses memorization until the contradiction has been resolved.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

The assistant should identify and explicitly resolve apparent contradictions.

---

# P007 — Preserve existing reasoning before correcting it

## Confidence

High

## Description

Lou often arrives with partially correct reasoning.

She benefits most when the explanation validates the correct part of her reasoning before explaining where it becomes incorrect.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Corrections should repair reasoning rather than replace it.

---

# P008 — Exact wording matters

## Confidence

High

## Description

Lou pays close attention to wording differences in QCMs.

She frequently asks why seemingly similar statements produce different answers.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Explanations should explicitly discuss wording when it changes the expected answer.

---

# P009 — High-yield synthesis is preferred over traditional summaries

## Confidence

High

## Description

Lou does not simply want shorter chapters.

She wants synthesis organized around recurring EDN concepts, reasoning patterns and frequently tested ideas.

## Evidence

- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Learning assets should prioritize exam relevance over chapter compression.

---

# P010 — Visual-spatial concepts require dedicated support

## Confidence

Medium

## Description

Lou experiences greater difficulty with anatomy and spatial reasoning than with conceptual mechanisms.

She repeatedly asks for concrete orientation and visualization.

## Evidence

- Lou-Ortho-01

Additional validation required.

## Product Implication

Generate dedicated visual learning assets for spatial concepts.

---

# P011 — Lou validates explanations by testing generalization

## Confidence

High

## Description

Lou considers an explanation successful only if it helps solve future unseen questions, not just the current example.

## Evidence

- Lou-Uro-02
- Lou-Ortho-01
- Lou-Gyneco-01

## Product Implication

Every explanation should help students recognize future variants of the same underlying concept.

---

# Research Notes

Patterns evolve over time.

Confidence should increase only when new conversations independently confirm the same behavior.

Patterns should remain stable and independent of any particular medical specialty.
