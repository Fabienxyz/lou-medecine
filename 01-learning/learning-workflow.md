# Learning Workflow

## Objective

Transform an official EDN paragraph into the most effective learning experience for Lou.

The official college never changes.

Only the learning experience changes.

---

## Phase 1

Global understanding

Outcome

"I understand where this paragraph fits inside the chapter."

---

## Phase 2

Deep understanding

Outcome

"I understand every important why."

---

## Phase 3

Long-term memorization

Outcome

"I can answer EDN questions reliably."

---

Each phase may generate several candidate learning assets.

Lou keeps the ones that work best for her.
