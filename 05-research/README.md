# Research

## Purpose

This folder contains all research conducted to discover how expert medical knowledge can be transformed into the most effective learning experience for medical students.

This is **not** product documentation.

This is **not** software architecture.

This is the scientific foundation of the project.

The objective is to understand:

- how medical colleges are structured;
- how medical knowledge is represented;
- which recurring patterns exist;
- where students struggle to understand;
- which pedagogical transformations consistently improve understanding.

The outputs of this research will later drive the product, architecture and AI implementation.

---

## Scope

Research focuses on three successive questions:

1. How is medical knowledge represented?
2. How should it be transformed to maximize understanding?
3. How should understanding later become long-term memory?

Only the first question is currently in scope.

---

## Corpus

Current corpus:

- SFCardio EDN College (Version 2024)

Future corpora:

- Other EDN colleges
- National recommendations
- Reference textbooks

---

## Guiding Principle

The corpus is the only source of truth.

Research describes the corpus before attempting to explain or improve it.

Architecture follows research.

Never the opposite.
