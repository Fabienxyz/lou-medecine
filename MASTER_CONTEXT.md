# MASTER_CONTEXT

## Project

Personal AI-assisted learning companion for Lou.

---

## Objective

Transform the official EDN colleges into learning materials adapted to Lou's way of understanding and studying.

The objective is to automate the preparation work while preserving complete fidelity to the college.

---

## Primary User

Lou

Second-year medical student.

Preparing for the EDN.

---

## Core Principles

- The college is the only source of medical knowledge.
- Medical knowledge is never modified.
- AI may generate pedagogical aids.
- Every medical statement is traceable.
- Trust always comes first.
