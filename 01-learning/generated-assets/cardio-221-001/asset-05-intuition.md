# Asset 05 — Intuition Before Biology

Forget medicine for one minute.

Imagine a brand-new road.

One day, small piles of rubbish start accumulating.

The city sends cleaning crews.

At first, everything seems under control.

But the rubbish keeps arriving.

The cleaners become overwhelmed.

More workers are called.

The area becomes crowded.

Traffic slows.

Eventually the city decides to build a concrete barrier around the rubbish to stop it spreading.

The barrier protects the rest of the road...

...but the road is now permanently narrowed.

That is almost exactly what happens during atherosclerosis.

| Story | Biology |
|--------|----------|
| Rubbish | Oxidized LDL |
| Cleaning crews | Macrophages |
| Workers accumulate | Foam cells |
| Continuous activity | Chronic inflammation |
| Concrete wall | Fibrous cap |
| Narrowed road | Atherosclerotic plaque |

---

## Key idea

The plaque is not the disease itself.

It is the visible scar left by years of chronic injury and repair.
