# Asset 03 — WHY Table

| Step | What happens? | Why? |
|-------|---------------|------|
| 1 | LDL enters the intima | Too much LDL crosses the endothelium and becomes trapped. |
| 2 | LDL is oxidized | Free radicals chemically modify LDL. |
| 3 | Endothelium reacts | Oxidized LDL is perceived as a danger signal. |
| 4 | Monocytes arrive | Adhesion molecules attract circulating immune cells. |
| 5 | Macrophages appear | Monocytes transform after entering the arterial wall. |
| 6 | Foam cells form | Macrophages ingest too much oxidized LDL. |
| 7 | Inflammation increases | Foam cells release inflammatory mediators. |
| 8 | Smooth muscle cells migrate | The vessel attempts to repair the lesion. |
| 9 | Fibrous cap forms | Collagen isolates the inflammatory lipid core. |
| 10 | Plaque exists | Repair + inflammation create a permanent lesion. |

---

## Central idea

The plaque is **not built intentionally**.

It is the consequence of the body's repeated attempts to repair chronic injury.
