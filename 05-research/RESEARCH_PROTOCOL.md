# Research Protocol

## Objective

Build an evidence-based understanding of how medical knowledge is represented in EDN colleges before designing any knowledge model or AI transformation pipeline.

---

# Principles

## 1. Corpus first

The medical college is the only source of evidence.

No concept should be introduced unless supported by repeated observations.

---

## 2. Observation before abstraction

Research follows four levels.

Facts

↓

Observations

↓

Patterns

↓

Hypotheses

Only validated hypotheses may influence architecture.

---

## 3. Evidence over intuition

Every observation must be traceable to one or more locations within the corpus.

No conclusion should rely on prior medical knowledge or personal intuition.

---

## 4. Separate observation from interpretation

Research notes must clearly distinguish:

- observed facts
- inferred patterns
- hypotheses
- recommendations

These must never be mixed.

---

# Research Process

The research progresses through successive passes over the corpus.

## Pass 1

Structural analysis

Question:

How is the document organized?

---

## Pass 2

Knowledge analysis

Question:

What kinds of information are represented?

---

## Pass 3

Pedagogical analysis

Question:

How are these concepts currently explained?

---

## Pass 4

Transformation opportunities

Question:

Which representations appear likely to improve understanding?

---

# Rules

During the research phase:

- no product decisions
- no architecture decisions
- no prompt engineering
- no implementation decisions

Research exists only to build evidence.

---

# Outputs

Research may produce:

- observations
- recurring patterns
- hypotheses
- research reports

No hypothesis becomes part of the platform until validated across multiple sections of the corpus.
